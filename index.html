How You Earn With Us

Barter is just the starting point. Here's how you can grow into a paid, percentage-based collaborator.

1

Trial Content (Barter)

Your first collaboration with a brand is a content trial — you receive the product on barter and create content to showcase it. No cash payment at this stage.

2

Performance Review

We track your content's reach and the orders generated through your shared link. Good reach + good orders = you move to the next stage automatically.

3

% Based Paid Collaboration

Once qualified, you automatically unlock percentage-based paid collaborations — for this brand and future brands. Track your status on the Payout page.
