Terms & Conditions

By submitting a barter collaboration request through this portal, you agree to the following terms:

1. Product Selection

Each influencer may select only one brand and one product per barter collaboration request.

2. Content Timeline & Permanent Removal Policy

Content must be created and submitted within 5 days of product delivery, via the "Submit Content" tab. If you do not submit within 5 days AND fail to inform our manager (collaboration@brandcrafter.tech), your name will be permanently disabled from our collaboration team.

3. Verification & Corrections

All submitted content is reviewed by our team. If corrections are needed, we will notify you with specific points to change. You will set your own timeline to complete the change, capped at a maximum of 3 days.

4. Earning Structure

Your first collaboration with any brand is a content trial on a barter-only basis. If your content's reach and orders generated are strong, you automatically qualify for percentage-based paid collaboration for that brand and future brands. No fixed script is provided — only key product highlight points.

5. Account & Login

Your account is accessed via your registered phone number and password. Password resets are verified via an emailed OTP.

6. Eligibility

Certain brand collaborations (e.g. Burkewala) have specific eligibility criteria which must be honestly disclosed.
